subtitle and aren't repeated in the keywords. Promotional text isn't searched.

## Description

Held Dear keeps the days that matter for the people you love on one line through your year: birthdays, anniversaries, Christmas, Mother's Day, Father's Day and the family days you add. When one comes close, it helps you plan the whole day and see when it's ready.

A PLAN FOR EACH DAY
Break each day into parts, like the gift, the card, the cake and the call. Each part shows who's doing it, you or the kids, and where it stands, from idea to ordered to done.

HOLIDAYS ON ONE PAGE
Christmas shows once on your year, not once per person, with everyone's plan on one page. Set up a tradition once, like an ornament for each child, and it comes back every year. Add your own holidays, such as Hanukkah, Lunar New Year or Diwali.

IDEAS WHEN THEY COME TO YOU
Save a gift idea by typing, or by sharing a link from any app. It waits on the person's page until the day. With Apple Intelligence, Held Dear can suggest gifts and write card lines on your iPhone.

CARDS
Make a card from your own photos to text or post, still or moving. Or mail a real paper card: pick a design, write your message, and it's written in pen, stamped and mailed for you. $7.99 per card with Apple Pay, to US addresses.

A LINE FOR EVERY YEAR
After each day, keep one line about what you did. Over the years it becomes a quiet record of how you showed up.

KIND AND PRIVATE
No streaks, red badges or guilt. If a day slips by, a late note still counts. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your people and plans stay on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you turn it on. Only a card you choose to mail is sent, and only to mail it.
Works with Larger Text, VoiceOver, Bold Text, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion, in light or dark.

FREE TO START
Birthdays from your contacts, reminders and the message on the day are free, with full plans for three people. Lifetime, $9.99 once, adds plans for everyone, anniversaries and life moments, every card design, every past year, widgets and Family Sharing. No subscription.
