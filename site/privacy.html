Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026. This covers Held Dear version 1.0 for iPhone.

In short: Held Dear has no account. Your people, plans and photos stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The only time anything leaves your phone is when you order a mailed card, and then only what's needed to mail it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your information is kept

  • On your iPhone. The people, dates, plans, ideas, notes and photos you add are stored in the app on your phone.
  • In your own iCloud, if you turn it on. Held Dear can keep a copy in your private iCloud so it is on your other Apple devices and backed up. That copy is in your Apple account, protected by Apple. The maker of Held Dear cannot see it.

What the app uses on your phone

  • Contacts: read to find names and birthdays. Held Dear writes to Contacts in one case only: adding a birthday to a contact that has none, when you tap "Add to Contacts". It never changes anything else.
  • Calendar: Held Dear can add an event when you ask it to. It does not read your calendar.
  • Photos: only the photos you choose. The app keeps its own copy.
  • Notifications: reminders are set on your phone. They are not sent from a server.
  • Gift ideas: suggestions are made on your phone by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent out.
  • Link previews: when you save a link, your phone asks that web page once for its title and picture. The page sees an ordinary visit. Nothing goes to Held Dear.

Mailed cards

When you order a handwritten card to be mailed, Held Dear sends what's needed to make and mail that one card, and nothing else about your people:

  • the card you chose and your message;
  • the recipient's name and mailing address;
  • your name and return address;
  • an Apple Pay payment token. Held Dear never sees your card number.

This goes through Held Dear's own server, which keeps none of it, to two companies that do the work:

  • Handwrytten writes and mails the card. It keeps order details (names, addresses and the message) for as long as Held Dear's account is open and for 3 years after, as its own privacy policy says. To have an order deleted sooner, email support@tangomike.app with the order number and we'll ask Handwrytten to delete it; they say they act within 30 days.
  • Stripe takes the payment and keeps the payment record, as payment law requires.

Card pictures in the app come through Held Dear's server too, so your phone only ever talks to Held Dear. This information is used only to mail your card and handle any refund, never for advertising or tracking.

Purchases

Held Dear Lifetime is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; the maker of Held Dear never sees your payment details.

Deleting your information

  • In the app: tap the circle at the top right of Home to open You, then Export and import, then "Delete all my data". This removes everything from your phone and from your iCloud copy.
  • Deleting the app removes the copy on your phone. To remove the iCloud copy as well, use "Delete all my data" first, or remove Held Dear's data in your iPhone's iCloud storage settings.

Children

Held Dear is not made for children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Anything new that leaves your phone will be described here before it is released.
