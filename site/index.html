Held Dear

Each birthday for the people you care about, turned into one finished, personal act, with a one-line record of every year.

What it does

  • Brings in birthdays from your contacts and shows who's coming up, left to right.
  • Plans each day as a few parts (a card, a call, a gift, a cake), each with who's doing it and when.
  • Keeps ideas and wishes for each person, so a good idea in March is there in December.
  • Groups Christmas, Mother's Day and Father's Day into one page.
  • Keeps a short line for each year, so you can see what you did and how it went.

Private by design

No account and no server. Your people, dates and photos stay on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you turn it on. Held Dear collects nothing.
