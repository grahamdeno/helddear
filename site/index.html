Christmas, Mother's Day and Father's Day on one page.

Shared holidays show once on your year, not eight times. Open Christmas and every person's plan is there together, so the stockings, the gifts and the gifts from the kids stay in step.

Anniversaries link two people, so a couple's day is planned once. Add your own family days and traditions, and Held Dear gives you one heads-up six weeks ahead.

Save the idea when it comes to you.

Someone mentions a pottery class in March. Save it to them in a second, by typing, by voice, or by sharing a link from any app. When their day comes, the idea is waiting, with the page's picture beside it.

Short on ideas? Held Dear suggests some on your phone from what you've saved about the person, and shows finds from small makers.

Questions

Does Held Dear find birthdays for me?

Yes. It reads the birthdays already saved in your contacts, and you choose who matters. You can also add anyone by hand. Held Dear never changes a contact, except to add a birthday when you ask it to.

Is it only for birthdays?

No. Anniversaries, Christmas, Mother's Day, Father's Day and your own family days all live on the same year, each with its own plan.

Can my partner and I both use it?

Lifetime works for your whole family through Family Sharing. Each person keeps their own people and plans.

What happens if I get a new iPhone?

Turn on iCloud in Held Dear, sign in on the new phone with the same Apple account, and your people come across on their own.

What iPhone do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
