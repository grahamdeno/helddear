Common questions

Where is my information kept?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you turn it on in the app (You, then iCloud). See the privacy policy.

How do I get my people onto a new phone?

Turn on iCloud in Held Dear (You, then iCloud) on your old phone. Sign in to the same Apple account on the new phone and install Held Dear. Your people come across on their own.

Why didn't a reminder come?

Check that notifications for Held Dear are on in your iPhone's Settings, under Notifications.

I bought Lifetime. How do I get it on another device?

In Held Dear, open You and tap "Restore purchase". Lifetime also works with Family Sharing.

How does Held Dear work?

In the app, open You and tap "How Held Dear works" for a one-page guide.

How do I delete everything?

In Held Dear, open You, then Export and import, then "Delete all my data". It asks before deleting.
