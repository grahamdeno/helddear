Terms of sale and refunds

Effective November 24, 2026. These terms cover mailed cards ordered in the Held Dear app.

In short: a mailed card costs $7.99, including the stamp. If it can't be sent, you're refunded automatically. If it hasn't arrived 10 business days after it was mailed, we'll send it again or refund it, your choice.

Who you're buying from

Mailed cards are sold by Graham D., the maker of Held Dear. Cards are handwritten and mailed by Handwrytten, a card service in the United States. Payment is taken with Apple Pay and processed by Stripe. On your statement it shows as HELDDEAR CARD.

What you get

  • One card you choose in the app, with your message handwritten in ink, mailed by US First-Class Mail to one address in the United States.
  • The price shown before you pay, including the stamp. Any tax is shown before you pay.
  • Cards are usually mailed within 1 to 3 business days. USPS usually delivers in 2 to 7 days. The app shows an arrival estimate and, in December, the last day to order for Christmas.

Refunds

  • If a card can't be placed, the payment is refunded automatically and the app tells you.
  • If a card hasn't arrived 10 business days after it was mailed, email us and we'll send it again or refund it. You choose.
  • Once a card is mailed, a change of mind isn't refunded, because it was handwritten and posted for that person.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank may take a few days to show them.

Your message and addresses

  • You're responsible for the message and the addresses you enter. Check them before you pay; a card can't be changed once it's being written.
  • Cards can't be used to harass, threaten or mislead anyone. We may refuse or cancel an order that does, and refund it.
  • How your names, addresses and message are handled is in the privacy policy.

Problems and questions

Email support@tangomike.app with the order number shown in the app. A person will reply.

Changes

If these terms change, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. The terms in place when you paid apply to your order.
