# Held Dear

Held Dear turns each birthday for the people you care about into one finished, personal act, and keeps a one-line
record of every year. Private by default: everything stays on your iPhone, with no account.

- **Setup in under two minutes:** birthdays from Contacts, pick your inner ring, allow alerts.
- **Capture in five seconds:** "Mike, March 3", or share a link to save a wish for someone.
- **Heads-up sized to the relationship:** 3 weeks for your inner ring, 1 week for the middle ring, the day for everyone else.
- **One tap on the day:** a photo message or a call, opened straight from the alert.
- **A record of every year:** what you did, a photo, and one line.
- **No guilt:** a missed birthday gets "A late note still counts", never a red badge.

Built with SwiftUI and SwiftData for iOS 26. See CLAUDE.md for how to build and test.
